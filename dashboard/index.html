Methodology

How to read the board

The screen

Candidates record no token in the snapshot and are screened out if they are a wrapped, staked, bridged, or versioned product of one that already trades.

The model

Multiples come from the median market-cap-to-TVL of token-bearing peers in the same category from this snapshot, with extreme ratios pulled back, thin categories shrunk toward the global median, then tilted by growth and tokenless age.

The plans

Each farm plan is written for its category with working-capital bands, expected costs, and time so the board shows what repeated usage could actually look like.
